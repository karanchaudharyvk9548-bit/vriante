About Us

THE VRIANTE STORY

From a small dream in Agra to a brand made for the uncommon.

WEAR YOUR ATTITUDE

Vriante was born in 2025 from a humble family in Agra with one simple dream — to create something of our own. No shortcuts, no support, just pure passion and the will to do something different.

We started with a simple idea: premium clothing shouldn't be a luxury. Every piece we craft — from oversized tees to tailored pants, from bold hoodies to classic shirts — is made for those who refuse to blend in.

Made For Uncommon isn't just a tagline. It's a promise.
